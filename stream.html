    How the stream learns

    Difficulty focus varies targeted draws from 0% to 60% (30% by default), revisiting words with at least three tries and two recent troubled attempts. Clean repeats gradually reduce that attention. Corrected mistakes still count. The dots show your last 20 tries, oldest to newest.

    This mix deliberately changes as you practice, so overall accuracy is not a fixed difficulty test. The broad-practice rate is shown separately. Timings exclude attempts interrupted by a long pause or leaving the page. Improvement is something to observe, not a score the app promises.
